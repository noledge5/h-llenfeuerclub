## Channels

| Channel | Behaviour | Default fade |
|---|---|---|
| Musik | 1 track, loops, cross-fade | 3 s |
| Atmosphäre | up to 3 loops, own volume each | 2 s |
| Effekte | one-shot, grid of 8 to 12, keys 1 to 9 | none |

Panic button "Alles ausblenden": 2 s fade to silence. Music ducks while the Vorlesen block is active.

## Event sounds (optional)

Laterne flackert: short hiss. Laterne erlischt: low tone. Welle 2 kommt: crack. Heldin fällt auf 0 TP: dull thud. Natürliche 20: fanfare.
